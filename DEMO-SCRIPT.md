# 60-Second Portfolio Demo Script

## Before recording

1. Open **Menu → Calibrate gestures** in bright, even lighting.
2. Open **Menu → Portfolio showcase demo → Open editable**.
3. Use the rear camera if the front-camera background is distracting.
4. Keep the phone cool and choose **Smooth** only when tracking remains stable.

## Recording flow

**0–06s — Hook**  
Show the live camera workspace and say: “This is a native Android spatial editor controlled by hand gestures.”

**06–16s — Natural creation**  
Switch to Smart Ink. Roughly draw a square or write a short word, then release the pinch so it cleans automatically.

**16–27s — True 3D**  
Select the central 3D object, switch to Transform, and rotate on X, Y, then Z.

**27–38s — Professional editing**  
Multi-select three objects. Open Style Studio, apply Neon Glass, then use Arrange to align or distribute them.

**38–48s — Hands-only interaction**  
Hold a V sign to open the gesture palette. Hover and pinch to select a mode. Perform one reliable palm swipe for undo or redo.

**48–56s — Presentation**  
Open full-screen presentation and use the air laser or fist pan.

**56–60s — Proof**  
Show Project Insights, then export the Portfolio Showcase Poster. End on “Built by Mohnish Raj.”

## Recording rule

Do not cut around failed gestures. Recalibrate or improve the build until the demo works naturally in one take.
